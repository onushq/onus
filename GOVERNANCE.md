Maintainers review and merge pull requests, triage issues, cut releases and keep the roadmap.

## How decisions are made

- Day-to-day changes are decided in pull requests by maintainer review.
- Significant decisions (a new dependency in the core, a change to a public schema, a new phase, anything hard to reverse) get an Architecture Decision Record in [docs/adr](docs/adr), proposed in a pull request and open for comment for at least a few days.
- The project lead has the final say while the project is small. As the contributor base grows, this document will move to a group of maintainers deciding by lazy consensus.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who have made sustained, high-quality contributions and reviews can be invited to become maintainers by the existing maintainers.

## Changes to this document

Changes to governance are made by pull request and need the project lead's approval.
